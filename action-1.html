01✳

Ask what would help.

Use surveys and small-group discussions to learn what students need most: classroom materials, tutoring, counselors, or something we haven’t heard yet.

02✳

Choose clear priorities.

Look for shared needs, agree on specific requests, and collect student stories. We would ask permission before sharing anyone’s experience.

03✳

Build visible support.

Organize a petition around those priorities. Share it through the website, planned social accounts, and campus events, with clear instructions for participating.

This is our campaign plan; a petition and survey are not open yet.
