01✳

Put education within reach.

We advocate for increased education funding and greater awareness of the costs students face. Opportunity should not depend on what a family can afford.

02✳

Fund what students need.

Updated classroom materials, accessible tutoring, school counselors, and opportunities for higher-level learning.

03✳

Ease the burden.

We are concerned that funding cuts can weaken programs and put pressure on school budgets. Students should not have to carry those pressures through higher costs and more borrowing.
