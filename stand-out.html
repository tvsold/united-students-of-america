01✳

Make the message travel.

We plan to create social media accounts, short videos, and trend-inspired posts that connect education funding to young people’s lives.

02✳

Every pace belongs.

Our proposed campus 5K would cost $5 to enter. Walk, jog, or run at your own pace. Distance markers would also explain why education funding matters.

Students in sports are part of our community too. This event would bring different campus groups into the conversation.

03✳

Find your people. Grow the cause.

We would reach out to sororities, fraternities, and student organizations about joint events. Their campus connections and social media presence could help more students join in.
